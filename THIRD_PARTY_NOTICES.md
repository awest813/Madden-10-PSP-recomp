The original runtime's MIT notice is retained in [LICENSE](LICENSE), including copyright 2026 Samir Nuri. The inherited God of War demo files and runtime history remain attributed to that source project; they are not NCAA gameplay evidence.

[PSPRecomp](https://github.com/jessicanataliagta/PSPRecomp) supplies the static compiler and core runtime under its MIT license, copyright 2026 PSPRecomp contributors. `scripts/setup.sh` fetches the pinned base and applies this project's patches. The fetched framework is excluded from the upload snapshot; its own license remains in that checkout. Patches 1–5 retain their original author metadata; patches 6–7 preserve the local NCAA/compiler/diagnostic changes.

`profile/third_party/at3_standalone` contains PPSSPP's standalone copy of FFmpeg's ATRAC3/ATRAC3+ decoder sources. These retain their per-file notices and GNU LGPL 2.1-or-later licensing. The complete license is [COPYING.LGPLv2.1](profile/third_party/at3_standalone/COPYING.LGPLv2.1), and the source revision is recorded in `profile/third_party/at3_standalone/SOURCE_COMMIT`. See [the third-party README](profile/third_party/README.md) for the small compatibility headers in `at3_shim`. The project-wide MIT license does not replace this decoder license.

Emscripten, ANGLE and the official PPSSPP reference installation are development/runtime dependencies installed separately. Their local SDKs and binaries are excluded from the source snapshot.

`profile/third_party/coi_serviceworker` vendors the unmodified [coi-serviceworker](https://github.com/gzuidhof/coi-serviceworker) at commit `7b1d2a092d0d2dd2b7270b6f12f13605de26f214`, under the MIT license, copyright 2021 Guido Zuidhof. Its [LICENSE](profile/third_party/coi_serviceworker/LICENSE) and `SOURCE_COMMIT` are retained. NCAA, EA and Sony marks identify the target and platform; this project has no affiliation or endorsement from them. Source contributions and synthetic tests are distinct from the local game-derived build outputs.

`profile/host/ge_reciprocal_data.hpp` records factual reciprocal coefficients fitted independently to normalized outputs of the pinned PPSSPP `61e60ee6ff` reference function. The [GE arithmetic documentation](https://www.ppsspp.org/docs/psp-hardware/gpu/arithmetic/) supplies the measured relation and hardware provenance. The port’s integer arithmetic is independently implemented; reference source and oracle-only harnesses remain local development evidence. This is reference-output agreement, not a new physical PSP measurement.

`profile/host/ge_setup_reciprocal_data.hpp` contains factual setup slopes independently fitted to 65,536 normalized outputs of the same pinned PPSSPP reference; setup starts follow the published arithmetic formula with its stated first-entry exception. `ge_plane_math.hpp` and `ge_plane_shader.hpp` independently implement the documented fixed-point raster stages. Reference-only source and oracle harnesses stay outside the published source tree. See the [GE arithmetic](https://www.ppsspp.org/docs/psp-hardware/gpu/arithmetic/) and [raster pipeline](https://www.ppsspp.org/docs/psp-hardware/gpu/raster-pipeline/) documentation for hardware provenance. No new physical PSP measurement is claimed.

`profile/translator` independently implements retail PSP executable decryption (PRX decrypt type 2, KIRK commands 1 and 7) with its own AES-128, AES-CMAC and SHA-1. The KIRK key-vault slot 0x5D, the KIRK command 1 header key and the per-tag seeds for tags `0xD91605F0`–`0xD91628F0` are factual key data published by the PSP research community and documented in PPSSPP's and JPCSP's loaders; the algorithm was implemented from that public documentation, and no GPL decrypter source is included. Its browser build links the unchanged PSPRecomp code generator (MIT). The translator produces game-derived output only on the user's device from the user's own disc.
